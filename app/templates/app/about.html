About MamloFoods

Transforming peanut processing through sustainable innovation while empowering local farming communities.

2024

Founded

Our Mission

To revolutionize peanut processing through sustainable technology while creating lasting positive impact in farming communities. We're committed to empowering smallholder farmers and promoting environmental sustainability through our solar-powered micro-factories.

Our Vision

A world where sustainable agriculture empowers communities, where technology serves both people and planet, and where quality food production uplifts rather than depletes our environment.
