Founder's Story

Leading with purpose

Irene Etyang is the Founder and CEO of MAMLO FOODS, a purpose-driven enterprise revolutionizing Africa’s peanut value chain through proximate solar-powered micro-factories. With a strong foundation in Food Science and Technology and over a decade of experience in product development, Irene blends technical expertise with a deep passion for empowering women in agriculture.

Under her visionary leadership, MAMLO FOODS has become a trailblazer in community-centered food processing—reducing waste, enhancing traceability, and increasing rural women’s incomes. By transforming smallholder farmers into producers and co-owners, Irene ensures that value remains within communities.

Her commitment to inclusive growth has earned her global recognition as a Mandela Washington Fellow, Leaders in Innovation Fellow, MB100 Awardee (2021), and more. Through MAMLO FOODS, Irene is creating a system where every product nourishes consumers and uplifts the hands that make it possible.
