Empowering Farmers Through Peanut Processing

MamloFoods is revolutionizing the peanut processing sector by empowering local farmers through innovation and access to modern markets. Our programs focus on training, quality assurance, and value addition to boost farmer income and sustainability.

We've launched several initiatives across rural Kenya to teach post-harvest handling, increase yields, and reduce spoilage. With our new facility, we're now able to support over 2,000 farmers, directly impacting food security and local economies.

Join us as we continue this journey — making agriculture a source of pride and prosperity.
