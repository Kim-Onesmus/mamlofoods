Founder's Story

Leading with purpose

Irene Etyang is the Founder and CEO of MAMLO FOODS, a purpose-driven enterprise revolutionizing Africa’s peanut value chain through proximate solar-powered micro-factories. With a strong foundation in Food Science and Technology and over a decade of experience in product development, Irene blends technical expertise with a deep passion for empowering women in agriculture. Under her visionary leadership, MAMLO FOODS has become a trailblazer in community-centered food processing, implementing innovative solutions that reduce waste, enhance traceability, and boost incomes for rural women farmers. By transforming smallholder farmers into producers and co-owners, Irene is ensuring that value stays within communities. Her commitment to inclusive growth and sustainable innovation has earned her global recognition as a Mandela Washington Fellow, Leaders in Innovation Fellow, MB100 Awardee (2021), Leading African Women in Food Fellow, and Mulago Fellow. Through MAMLO FOODS, Irene is creating a system where every product nourishes consumers and uplifts the hands that make it possible.

Our Core Values

INNOVATION

We leverage cutting-edge technology, like solar-powered micro-factories, to revolutionize local peanut processing and enhance community-driven value addition.

COLLABORATIONS

We partner with like-minded organizations to foster a shared ownership model that empowers women smallholder farmers to actively participate in decision-making and benefit from the business’s success.

SUSTAINABLE PRACTICES

Sustainability is at the core of our operations, from using renewable energy in our factories to promoting eco-friendly farming techniques that ensure long-term environmental and community health.
